📚 POKÉMON ENCYCLOPEDIA

What is Pokémon?

Pokémon (short for Pocket Monsters) is a worldwide franchise centered on fictional creatures called "Pokémon". Humans, known as Pokémon Trainers, catch, collect, and train them to battle each other for sport, bonding, and friendship!

When was it created?

Pokémon was officially born on February 27, 1996, with the launch of the video games Pokémon Red and Green for the original Nintendo Game Boy in Japan.

How was it created? (The Game Freak Origins)

The franchise was created by Satoshi Tajiri. As a child living in Machida, Tokyo, Satoshi loved exploring forests and catching insects. Over time, as Japan became more urbanized, the forests disappeared, and children began playing indoors.

When Satoshi saw children playing with Nintendo Game Boys, he envisioned virtual insects crawling across the Game Boy's Link Cable. He founded the independent studio Game Freak with his friend Ken Sugimori (who designed all of the original 151 monsters). With crucial mentorship from Mario's creator, Shigeru Miyamoto, they spent 6 long, difficult years developing "Capsule Monsters" (later shortened to Pocket Monsters) and nearly went bankrupt before releasing their masterpiece!

The Nintendo Toy & Game Boy Connection

Did you know Nintendo was founded way back in 1889 as a handmade playing card company? Before making video games, they designed classic toys in Japan! Years later, Satoshi Tajiri combined Nintendo's tech with his childhood love of bug-hunting. He realized the Nintendo Game Boy's Link Cable (which was used to connect two devices together) could let players physically trade creatures with friends—just like trading real toys or card collectables. This awesome concept sparked a global revolution of collectible figures, plushies, and mechanical Pokéball toys!

The Rarest Pokémon Cards

The Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) launched in late 1996 and features some of the most sought-after collector items in history:

⭐ Pikachu Illustrator: The absolute holy grail! Only 39 were given out as prizes to winners of an art contest in Japan in 1997-1998.

🔥 1st Edition Shadowless Charizard: The legendary holographic card from the 1999 Base Set. Its clean "shadowless" borders make it highly prized by classic fans.

⚡ Prerelease Raichu: A mythic card printed with a gold "Prerelease" stamp by accident during early production. Only a tiny handful exist!
